# Finance Month-End Close Checklist

A practical control checklist for validating that the Odoo finance operating model is ready for a controlled monthly close.

> Adapt this checklist to the applicable accounting standards, local tax rules, statutory requirements, company policy, and Odoo localization.

## 1. Cut-Off & Period Control

- [ ] Accounting period identified
- [ ] Transaction cut-off communicated
- [ ] Prior-period lock dates reviewed
- [ ] Unauthorized back-dated posting tested/blocked
- [ ] Unposted draft transactions reviewed

## 2. Accounts Receivable

- [ ] Customer invoices reviewed
- [ ] Credit notes reviewed
- [ ] Customer receipts posted
- [ ] Unallocated receipts investigated
- [ ] Customer balances reconciled
- [ ] Receivable aging reviewed
- [ ] Major overdue items explained

## 3. Accounts Payable

- [ ] Vendor bills reviewed
- [ ] Vendor credit notes reviewed
- [ ] Vendor payments posted
- [ ] Unmatched vendor transactions investigated
- [ ] Vendor balances reconciled
- [ ] Payable aging reviewed

## 4. Cash & Banks

- [ ] Cash journals counted/reconciled
- [ ] Bank statements imported/entered
- [ ] Bank transactions reconciled
- [ ] Unreconciled bank lines investigated
- [ ] Bank GL balances reconcile to statements
- [ ] Outstanding payments/deposits reviewed

## 5. General Ledger

- [ ] Suspense accounts reviewed
- [ ] Clearing accounts reviewed
- [ ] Intercompany balances reviewed where applicable
- [ ] Manual journal entries approved
- [ ] Accruals posted
- [ ] Prepayments reviewed
- [ ] Recurring journals reviewed
- [ ] Reclassification entries posted

## 6. Fixed Assets

- [ ] Asset additions reviewed
- [ ] Disposals reviewed
- [ ] Depreciation generated
- [ ] Depreciation posted
- [ ] Asset register reconciles to GL

## 7. Inventory / Costing — If Applicable

- [ ] Inventory movements reviewed
- [ ] Negative stock exceptions reviewed
- [ ] Inventory valuation reconciled
- [ ] Stock adjustment entries approved
- [ ] Inventory GL agrees to valuation report

## 8. Payroll — If Integrated

- [ ] Payroll finalized
- [ ] Payroll accounting entry posted
- [ ] Net payroll reconciled
- [ ] Payroll liabilities reconciled
- [ ] Payroll GL totals agree to approved payroll register

## 9. Tax & Statutory Controls

- [ ] Sales tax/VAT accounts reviewed
- [ ] Purchase tax/VAT accounts reviewed
- [ ] Withholding accounts reviewed where applicable
- [ ] Tax reports reconciled to ledger
- [ ] Statutory outputs reviewed by responsible finance/tax owner

## 10. Financial Statements

- [ ] Trial balance generated
- [ ] Total debit = total credit
- [ ] Profit & Loss reviewed
- [ ] Balance Sheet reviewed
- [ ] Cash-flow reporting reviewed where applicable
- [ ] Material variances explained
- [ ] Management dimensions/analytics reconciled

## 12. Post-Close

- [ ] Period locked according to policy
- [ ] Final reports archived
- [ ] Reconciliation evidence archived
- [ ] Open issues transferred to next-period action log
- [ ] Material post-close adjustments governed through approved process

## Readiness Rule

A successful ERP go-live should not be considered financially stable until the organization can complete at least one controlled month-end close with reconciled balances, documented exceptions, approved financial outputs, and repeatable ownership.
